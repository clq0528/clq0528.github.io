Statistical Analysis Report (2025)

Results: A one-sample t-test was conducted to compare the heights of 10-year-old boys (N=100) in 2025 to a baseline of 138.0 cm.

The analysis revealed that the mean height (M = 139.63, SD = 4.99) was significantly higher than the hypothesized value, t(99) = 3.26, p = .002. The 95% confidence interval for the mean was [138.64, 140.62].

Conclusion: Although statistically significant, the effect size (Cohen's d = 0.30) suggests a small practical impact.
